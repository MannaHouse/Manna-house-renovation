The Vision

A Space That Reflects
the Peace of Christ

To build a home for the sojourner, we want our physical space to reflect the hospitality and peace of Christ. We are undertaking a $20,000 cosmetic renovation of our main gathering space at Dilworth Baptist Church.

Our historic 45×60ft sanctuary has served this neighborhood faithfully. Now we want to transform it into a place where the exhausted can find rest, where the wandering can find roots — and where every person who walks in feels, before a word is spoken, that they belong here.

Thanks to our church partners who have already sent teams and recommendations, we have a clear, costed plan to make this vision a reality.

Scope of Work

Three Phases.
One Transformed Space.

Phase 01

The Canvas

$6,450

Removing old partitions, repairing the ceiling, and executing a professional spray-paint of the entire room in a bright, clean white — a blank canvas for everything that follows.

Phase 02

Atmosphere & Lighting

$5,210

Installing 24 ultra-slim commercial-grade LED panel lights for clean, even illumination across the sanctuary, plus upgrading our electrical infrastructure to support the full vision.

Phase 03

Stage & Focal Point

$8,130

Expanding the stage with high-traffic contemporary gray carpet, a textured acoustic panel feature wall, warm indirect LED lighting, and elevated staging that sets the room's tone.
